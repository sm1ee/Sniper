                  Saved HTTP History limit
                  
                  
                  
                

                
Saving an enabled policy permanently deletes the oldest unprotected entries above the total target, now and as history grows. Deleted requests, responses and notes cannot be restored. Turning cleanup off does not restore deleted entries.

                
Original records referenced by durably saved HTTP tabs and in-progress stored records are protected. Protected entries count toward the total; if they exceed the target, the limit is soft. Unsaved tabs and note edits do not protect records.
